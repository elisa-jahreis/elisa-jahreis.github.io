01 / About

About Me

I am Elisa Jahreis, a marine scientist with an M.Sc. in Marine Ecosystem and Fisheries Sciences from the University of Hamburg, Germany.

My scientific background combines tropical coral reef ecology, coral thermal tolerance experiments, marine fieldwork, and environmental data analysis. During my master's research in Fiji, I investigated coral responses to thermal stress using the Coral Bleaching Automated Stress System (CBASS), alongside field-based reef research.

My professional experience spans tropical reef environments, European marine research institutions, and a remote marine research station on Canada's Pacific coast. Most recently, I worked as a Research Coordinator at the Bamfield Marine Sciences Centre, supporting researchers, field operations, laboratory infrastructure, and scientific logistics.

I am particularly interested in connecting field observations and experimental ecology with spatial environmental data. My current professional development focuses on GIS, satellite remote sensing, and their applications in coral reef monitoring, marine habitat assessment, and conservation planning.

GIS Project 01 · In Development

Mapping Environmental Exposure of Coral Reefs along Fiji's Coral Coast

A geospatial analysis project focused on mapping coral reef habitats and environmental exposure in the coastal region of Votua, Fiji.

The planned workflow will explore reef habitat mapping, satellite-derived environmental information, and spatial patterns relevant to reef monitoring and conservation planning.

Potential data sources include Allen Coral Atlas, Sentinel-2 imagery, and NOAA Coral Reef Watch. Data suitability and spatial resolution will be evaluated before analysis.
