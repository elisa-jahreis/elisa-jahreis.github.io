Background

About me

I hold an M.Sc. in Marine Ecosystems and Fisheries Sciences from the University of Hamburg. My work spans coral reef ecology, experimental marine research, field operations and research coordination.

My research includes investigating coral thermal tolerance and bleaching responses in Fiji. Most recently, I worked as a Research Coordinator at the Bamfield Marine Sciences Centre in Canada, supporting visiting researchers, marine field operations and scientific infrastructure.

I am now building skills in Geographic Information Systems (GIS), remote sensing and marine spatial planning to connect ecological evidence with spatial analysis and conservation applications.
